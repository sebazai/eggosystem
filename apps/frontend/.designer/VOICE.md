# Voice & copy rules

## Person

- **Second person** is default to the reader: _"Build your fantasy team"_, _"Watch your favorite teams"_.
- **First‑person plural** is the organization's own voice: _"Since 2018, we've been pioneering corporate esports"_.
- **Never first‑person singular.**

## Casing

- **Title Case** in navigation, section headers, and button labels.
  - _"View Match Calendar"_, _"Browse Teams"_, _"Hall of Fame"_, _"Register Your Team"_
- **Sentence case** in descriptions and in‑product copy.
- **ALL CAPS** is reserved for the display face only:
  - The logo's "CORPORATE ESPORTS LEAGUE" eyebrow
  - The match `LIVE` pill
  - Eyebrow section kickers
  - **Don't ALL‑CAPS body copy.**
- Finnish product names stay Finnish: **Kanaliiga, Kanahub, Kanahautomo**. Don't translate them.

## Tone signals

- Short directional CTAs with a right‑arrow or chevron: _"Register Your Team →"_, _"View Calendar"_.
- Comfortable with numbers: _"85 organizations · 700+ players · 96 teams"_. Stats lead.
- **Bolds** inside paragraphs carry the emphasis. Italics are rare.
- Emoji are used sparingly and only as a leading glyph on a section header (see `ICONOGRAPHY.md`).

## Examples to match

> Building Corporate Culture Through Esports

> Since 2018, Kanaliiga has been pioneering corporate esports in Finland, bringing together **over 2 500 professionals from 300+ companies** annually.

> 🎮 CS2 Season 4 – Live Now

> Finland's Premier **Corporate Esports League**

## Never

- Corporate jargon: "leverage", "synergies", "bandwidth", "circle back".
- Apologies: "sorry for the inconvenience".
- Exclamation‑mark spam.
- Marketing adjectives without a number behind them.
